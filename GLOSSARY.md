# Discover Tarkarli

A destination marketplace for the Tarkarli / Malvan coast: travellers find and book stays, food, shops and experiences from local businesses, alongside free guides to the area.

## Who is on the platform

**Platform**:
discovertarkarli.com itself, run by the Owner. It lists Partners and Public listings and publishes Guides.
_Avoid_: Site, marketplace (when you mean the business)

**Partner**:
A private business listed on the Platform, such as a homestay, resort, kitchen or shop. A Partner is chargeable, even while its listing is free.
_Avoid_: Tenant (in product language), vendor, merchant, operator

**Partner application**:
A business's own request to join the Platform, which it starts itself. It becomes a Partner only when the Platform approves it.
_Avoid_: Sign-up, registration

**Partner type**:
The kind of business a Partner is: Stay, Food, Shop, and later Experience and Rental. It decides which features the Partner gets.
_Avoid_: Category (reserved for traveller-facing browsing)

**Public listing**:
An offering run by the government or a non-profit, such as a state-run scuba centre or a conservation group. It is listed free, permanently, and is never a Partner.
_Avoid_: Free partner, government partner

**Place of interest**:
A beach, fort, temple, viewpoint or other spot with no owner on the Platform. It is pure content.
_Avoid_: Attraction, POI listing

**Partner site**:
A Partner's own branded booking page on the Platform, or on the Partner's own domain. Guests book and pay here, with the Partner as Merchant of record.
_Avoid_: Microsite, storefront

**Listing**:
A Partner's or Public listing's presence in Platform search and browse: photos, details, prices and contact buttons. A Stay's listing leads to its Partner site. Other listings end in an enquiry.
_Avoid_: Profile, page

**Enquiry**:
A traveller contacting a Partner from a Listing, by WhatsApp or by phone. It is counted as Usage.
_Avoid_: Lead

**Trip plan**:
A traveller's shortlist of Listings and Places of interest across several Partners. It has no combined payment; each booking is with one Partner.
_Avoid_: Cart, basket, itinerary

**Guide**:
Platform-written content about the area: how to reach Malvan, places of interest, seasons and activities.
_Avoid_: Blog, article

**Verified**:
A badge on a Partner whose licence or approval the Platform has checked. It confers trust, not free listing.
_Avoid_: Government approved (as a listing tier)

## Money

**Merchant of record**:
The party a guest pays and whose name is on the guest's receipt. Today that is always the Partner, never the Platform.
_Avoid_: Seller (ambiguous)

**Payment account**:
How a Partner receives guest money: pay at property, the Partner's own UPI ID, or (later) the Partner's own Razorpay account.
_Avoid_: Gateway, merchant account

**Tax profile**:
A Partner's GST standing: unregistered, registered (regular) or composition. It decides what tax appears on prices and receipts.
_Avoid_: GST mode

**Featured**:
Paid, prominent placement of a Partner, on top of its free basic listing.
_Avoid_: Sponsored, promoted, premium listing

**Usage**:
Measured activity a Partner generates on the Platform (views, enquiries, bookings, messages). It is recorded from day one so the Platform can charge for it later.
_Avoid_: Telemetry (in product language), analytics

## People and access

**Owner**:
The person who owns discovertarkarli.com. The Owner holds every Platform permission, including billing.
_Avoid_: Super admin, root

**Platform admin**, **Content editor**, **Finance**:
Platform-side roles below the Owner. In order, they cover Partner approval and support, Guides and translations, and invoices and settlements.

**Partner owner**, **Manager**, **Staff**:
Partner-side roles. In order, they cover the payout account, plan and team; listings, prices, bookings and settings; and day-to-day bookings and check-in.

**Support session**:
A time-boxed, audited window in which a Platform user may see a Partner's guest data. This is the only way Platform staff see it.
_Avoid_: Impersonation, login-as
